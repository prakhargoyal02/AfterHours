# AfterHours — liquid listening
Music feels different together.

The listening scene is a dim room, a late walk, or two people separated by distance. Music and people lead; navigation recedes. Preserve the canvas contour and its real FFT input.

Palette: near-black graphite (#090b10), mercury (#e4e9ef), mist (#a6b0be), warm amber for Couple, cool rain for weather. Artwork supplies ambient colour without affecting control contrast. Manrope remains self-hosted. Display tracking stops at -.04em. No numbered decorative sections or fabricated online claims.

Discover is an atmospheric listening stage with immediately reachable search/mood controls and a track list. Now Playing expands into a protected fullscreen listening surface. Queue and temporary conversation use floating sheets. Desktop keeps a narrow navigation rail and generous listening stage; mobile uses a bottom navigation and compact dock above it.

Motion contract: GSAP owns reveals, sheet entry/exit and state choreography. Canvas retains real audio-energy geometry, driven by elapsed time, with focus damping, Couple warmth, Party gain and rain ripples. Paused is still; reduced motion is static. Stop painting when hidden. Use scoped cleanup; never animate inaccessible hidden controls.

Keyboard: native modal dialogs, Escape, focus return, labelled controls. Touch targets >=44px. Swipe only on dedicated handles/artwork, never hijack range inputs. Loading, provider error, empty, offline and disconnected states must describe reality.

Delivery: this document is direction, not a claim of completed features. Audit remains the baseline; tests distinguish mocked UI from live service integration.
